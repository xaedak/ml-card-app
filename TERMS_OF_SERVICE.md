## 2. What the Bot Does

The Bot lets a Discord user generate a custom "player card" image for the mobile game Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. To do this, it:

- Presents a dropdown menu of background designs to choose from
- Collects information you type into Discord's own pop-up forms (name to display, birthday, in-game player ID, server ID, roles played, main hero, highest rank)
- Combines that information with your Discord avatar and username to generate a single image
- Sends that image back to you privately and posts it to a channel chosen by the server's administrators
